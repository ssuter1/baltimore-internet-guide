# Contributing to the Baltimore City Residential Internet Guide

Thanks for helping keep this guide useful and current.

Baltimore internet availability can change block by block, and providers regularly change speeds, technologies, pricing, and service areas. Community reports are welcome.

## The easiest way to suggest a change

Open a GitHub Issue and include as much of the following as you can:

- Provider
- Baltimore neighborhood or general area, if relevant
- What information should be added or changed
- A link, screenshot, provider notice, or other source if available
- Whether the information is based on your own service or installation experience

You do not need to be a networking expert. Plain-English reports are useful.

## Pull requests

If you are comfortable with GitHub, you can edit `README.md` directly and submit a pull request.

Please try to:

- Keep the guide understandable to nontechnical readers.
- Explain technical terms when they are introduced.
- Avoid turning provider sections into advertisements.
- Distinguish provider claims from personal or community experiences.
- Avoid posting personal information, account numbers, exact home addresses, or other sensitive information.
- Keep address-specific availability claims appropriately qualified. Service may differ even between neighboring properties.

## What belongs in the guide?

Useful changes include:

- New residential ISPs serving Baltimore City
- New fiber or fixed-wireless buildouts
- Neighborhood expansion information
- Major changes in available speeds or access technology
- Retirement of older services
- Corrections to technical explanations
- Changes to FCC resources
- Baltimore-specific installation or availability information

Pricing changes frequently and may vary by promotion or address, so the guide generally focuses on technology, availability, and service characteristics rather than maintaining a detailed price list.

## Community reports

Firsthand reports can be valuable, especially for neighborhood buildouts and fixed-wireless availability. When something is based primarily on community experience rather than an official provider announcement, the guide should say so.

Thanks for helping make broadband information in Baltimore easier to understand.
